CRUNCHR 1.0.0
=============

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Unzip the folder, then double-click "crunchr.html".

It opens in your browser. There's nothing to install and no account to make.
Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.

If double-clicking opens the wrong app, right-click the file, choose "Open
with", and pick your browser.


WHAT IT IS
----------
Crunchr is a small sound tool for games. It makes clicks, footsteps, dice,
cloth, coins, squishes and crunches.

Pick one of the 11 sounds at the top, click it again when you want a fresh take,
and shape it with the controls until it sits right under the action in your
game. Every sound is built from scratch in the page, so there are no sample
files to keep track of.


MAKE IT YOURS
-------------
The gold Play button, the Spacebar and the sound wave all play the current
sound. The sliders change Length, Pitch, Punch, Body, Roughness and Fade.
Variation changes how different the ten downloadable versions are from one
another.

Reset in the Shape section puts the sliders back where they started. Nudge 10%
and Surprise me are at the top.


DOWNLOADING
-----------
"Download WAV" saves the sound you're hearing as one standard WAV file, and
it's 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo, which is what most game engines and editors
expect.

"Download 10 versions" saves a ZIP with ten numbered WAVs and small differences
between them. If you need a row of footsteps that don't repeat, the ZIP gives
you ten versions to scatter through the level instead of one file playing again
and again.

The row of numbers under Save plays the ten versions. Not sure which one to
keep? Click through the numbers and listen before you save. "Save vNN" keeps
just that file. The "Space between sounds" slider only changes the preview. But
the ZIP always has ten separate files, so your game decides when each one
plays.


IT WORKS OFFLINE
----------------
Everything the app needs is inside that one file. It opens on a plane or with
the wifi switched off.

When you close the tab and come back later, the sound you were working on and
the slider positions are still there, so you can pick up where you left off.
Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and the app doesn't connect to the
internet. Clearing the site data removes the saved sound.


BROWSER SUPPORT
---------------
Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Browsers only allow sound after a
click or key press, so press something first. On a phone or tablet the layout
stacks into one column, the controls get bigger, and tapping a sound plays it
right away, so it stays usable on a small screen.
