CRUNCHR 1.0.0
=============

HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Unzip this folder, then double-click "crunchr.html".

It opens in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account to make, and
no server involved. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.

If double-clicking opens the wrong app, right-click the file, choose "Open with",
and pick your browser.


WHAT IT IS
----------
Crunchr is a small instrument for making game sound effects: clicks, footsteps,
dice, cloth, coins, squishes and crunches.

Pick one of the 11 sounds at the top. Click it again to roll a fresh version
with new random detail. Shape it with the seven controls, then listen. Every
sound is synthesized from scratch in your browser, so there are no sample files
and nothing to download from us.


MAKE IT YOURS
-------------
The big gold Play button, the Spacebar and the sound wave all replay the current
sound. Shape it with Length, Pitch, Punch, Body, Roughness and Fade. Variation
changes how much the ten downloadable versions differ from one another. Reset in
the Shape section returns the sliders to the preset defaults. Nudge 10% and
Surprise me are in the sound bank at the top.


DOWNLOADING
-----------
"Download WAV" saves the sound on your sliders as one 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo
WAV file.

"Download 10 versions" saves a ZIP containing ten numbered WAVs, with small
differences between them. Those are useful for footsteps and clicks that should
not repeat identically.

The numbered strip under Save previews those ten versions. Click a number to hear
one, then "Save vNN" to download just that file. The "Space between sounds"
slider only changes the preview; the ZIP always contains ten separate files, so
your game decides when to trigger each one.


IT WORKS OFFLINE
----------------
Everything the app needs is inside that one HTML file. It opens on a plane or
with the wifi switched off.

Your last session (the selected sound, its sliders, version and preview gap) is
kept in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, no analytics
and no network requests. Clearing the site data removes the saved session.


BROWSER SUPPORT
---------------
Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari with Web Audio support. Browsers only
allow sound to start after a click or key press, so press a button first. On
phones and tablets the layout stacks into one column, and tapping a sound
auditions it.
